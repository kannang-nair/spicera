About Us

Our story

Spicera was born from a simple belief — that the almond from Kashmir, the cashew from Goa and the pepper from Malabar each carry the taste of their soil. We work directly with growers in India's GI-tagged regions, so every jar that reaches your home still smells of the farm it came from.
