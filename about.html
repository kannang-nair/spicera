Spicera is a one-stop shop for India's Geographical Indication (GI) and famous-origin harvests — the almond that only tastes like this in Kashmir, the pepper the world sailed for, the turmeric with the deepest gold.

Why origin matters

A Geographical Indication tag is India's promise that a product truly comes from the place that made it famous — and that its quality is inseparable from that soil, climate and craft. Byadagi chilli gets its deep red from Karnataka's dry winters; Alleppey cardamom earns its "Queen of Spices" crown in Kerala's high ranges; Erode turmeric carries curcumin levels other regions can't match.

We source each lot directly from growers in these regions, test it, pack it fresh in small batches, and ship it with its origin printed proudly on the label. No blends. No fillers. Just the harvest.
